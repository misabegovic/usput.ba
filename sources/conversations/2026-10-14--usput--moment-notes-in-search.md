# Moment notes in semantic search, 2026-10-14

Before building semantic search, the agent asked whether the notes travellers
write on their moments should be embedded. The options were to find a moment
through its place's text only, so travellers' notes never go to OpenAI or
Langfuse, or to embed the notes of approved public moments, with private and
pending moments never sent. Answer: "Yes, public moments only".
