# Charging for audio tours, 2026-09-30

> For usput, I want to have some sort of profit made maybe. Maybe based on
> audio tours? We need a way to charge money for them somehow.

Answers, asked one by one:

1. How travellers pay: "Subscription".
2. What it unlocks: "Audio paid, everything else free". Places, plans,
   walking, check-ins, moments and reviews stay free; subscribers get every
   audio tour, and each tour's first stop plays free as a preview.
3. Periods: weekly, monthly and yearly.
4. Who receives the money: "A company abroad", selling through Stripe.
5. Which country: "Not decided". Build against Stripe in test mode; the
   account country is set when the company exists.
6. Prices: EUR 3 a week, EUR 6 a month, EUR 39 a year.
7. Trials or codes: "None". Only the free first stop.
8. Renewal: "All plans renew", weekly included.
